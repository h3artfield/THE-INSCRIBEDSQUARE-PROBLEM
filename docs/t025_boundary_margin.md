# T025 Boundary Margin

---

## Statement

There exists explicit \(m>0\) such that

\[
|F(u)|_\infty \ge m \qquad \text{for every } u\in\partial B.
\]

---

## Method

Adaptive interval subdivision of all eight 3-dimensional faces of \(\partial B\):

- Fix one coordinate at lo/hi.
- Subdivide free dimensions until each terminal cell has at least one component of \(F\) with interval excluding zero.
- Record excluded component index and cell margin.

Certificate: `artifacts/t025_boundary_exclusion_certificate.json` (~1600 terminal cells at depth 6).

---

## Certified margin

\[
m = 2.10\times 10^{-5}
\]

(minimum over terminal cell component margins; independently recomputed by validator).

---

## Independent validation

`validate_boundary_certificate()`:

1. Reloads every terminal cell.
2. Reconstructs \(F\) via `iv_backend_v1`.
3. Confirms stated excluded component excludes zero.
4. Recomputes global margin.

Union coverage: cells generated by recursive subdivision from face anchors; no rational gaps between sibling subcells.

---

## Use in T027

Homotopy argument requires \(E(\eta_0) < m\). With \(E(\eta_0)\approx 6.76\times10^{-6}\), boundary nonvanishing persists for all \(C^0\) perturbations with \(\|q_i-p_i\|_\infty\le\eta_0\).
