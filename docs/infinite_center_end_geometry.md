# Infinite-Center End Geometry

Center cover number \(N_c(v,\eta)\): minimum \(\eta r(v)\)-scale regions covering cylinder mass.

`INFINITE_CENTER_BRANCHING_TREE` when \(N_c(v_j,\eta)\to\infty\) along positive-mass ends.

Relationship to `DIFFUSE_END_MEASURE_TREE`: infinite-center implies diffuse or positive-scale; not overlapping without stated implication.

**Module:** `infinite_center_end_geometry.py`
