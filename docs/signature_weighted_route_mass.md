# Signature-Weighted Route Mass (Directive 057)

[
M_r(n)=\sum_{v\in S_n}\alpha_r(v)\Phi(v),
\qquad
\sum_r M_r(n)=\sum_{v\in S_n}\Phi(v).
]

Slice pigeonhole: some route carries at least \(1/|\mathcal R|\) of total \(\Phi\)-signature mass.

**Module:** `route_signature_mass.py`, `route_responsibility.py`
