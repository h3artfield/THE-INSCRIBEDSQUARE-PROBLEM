# Admissible Neutral Transition Space (Directive 053)

Transition datum \(\mathfrak t=(X(v),\{X(w)\},A(v,w),E(v),\mathcal G(v))\) with geometric admissibility:

- `JORDAN_PARENT`, `JORDAN_CHILDREN`, `CHILD_HULL_COMPATIBILITY`
- `SINGLETON_END_COMPATIBILITY`, `CONNECTED_PARAMETER_SUPPORT`
- `NEUTRAL_SIGNATURE_BUDGET`, `VALID_GATE_ORDER`, `VALID_CROSSCUT_GEOMETRY`
- `FINITE_OR_MEASURE_CHILD_DATA`, `EXACT_STATE_RECURSION`

Algebraically feasible states are distinguished from geometrically realizable transitions.

**Module:** `admissible_neutral_transition.py`
