# T025 Compactness Proof — Audit Result

**Status:** **NOT VALID** for smooth curve (Directive 011R)

---

## Directive 011 claim

Compactness of truncation squares \(\Rightarrow\) square on infinite \(\Gamma_{\mathrm{ds}}\).

---

## Why compactness fails

1. Square \(Q\) is **fixed** for all \(N\ge 3\) — compactness reduces to a constant sequence \(v_{k,i}=v_i\).
2. Limit points \(v_i\) are **not** on smooth \(\gamma_+\) (chord points).
3. Limit of polygon boundaries \(\neq\) pointwise limit of chord-midpoint squares on smooth arc.

---

## What would repair T025

* Prove \(v_i\in\Gamma_{\mathrm{ds}}\) (currently false), **or**
* Validated interval root on smooth pieces with spurious-root exclusion, **or**
* Different branch with vertices on genuine curve pieces.
