# Infinite-Center Critical Geometry (Directive 048)

Center count \(N_c(v,\eta)\) tracks coherent regions per scale. Growth: POLYNOMIAL, EXPONENTIAL, DIFFUSE, BOUNDED.

Routes: quadratic packing (T087), diffuse incidence, parameter disconnection, positive-scale nonannular, non-Jordan, or critical residual.
