# T024-B — Strong Topology Obstruction

**Directive 011R clarification**

---

## Exact theorem (proved case)

Let \(Q_n:S^1\to\mathbb R^2\) be \(C^1\) and \(\|Q_n-\Gamma_{\mathrm{ds}}\|_{C^1(K)}\to 0\) on every compact subarc \(K\subset S^1\setminus\{\text{origin}\}\) with derivatives in arc-length coordinates.

Then **no** normalized Liouville primitives \(f_n(0)=0\) converge uniformly on a period if tail relative area \(\to\infty\) on shrinking intervals.

---

## Not proved

**\(C^0\) primitive obstruction:** Asano–Ike uses \(C^1\) approximants with **\(C^0\)** curve convergence and primitive convergence. T024-B does **not** rule out that route.

## Canonical curve caveat
