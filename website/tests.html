What we verify (and what we do not)

Pytest clean-room audits verify internal consistency of certificate functions on certified fixtures. They are not a proof of the universal Toeplitz conjecture. Floating-point oracles are test-only cross-checks.

Clean-room audits (not Lean)

The repository uses Python modules named *_clean_room.py that answer fixed audit questionnaires (curve-level definitions, scope separation, conjectural labels). Examples:

  • t103_clean_room.py — Directive 057 / coherent routes
  • directive_058_clean_room.py — global residual ledger
  • directive_059_clean_room.py — positive-scale geometry
  • directive_060_clean_room.py — non-Jordan saturation hulls
  • directive_061_clean_room.py — parameter-disconnected limits
