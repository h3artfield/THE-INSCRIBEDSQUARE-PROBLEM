\begin{abstract}
Jordan curves of finite $2$-variation inscribe every prescribed rectangle,
via simple polygonal interpolation \tagsource, the simple-arc area inequality
T021 \taglocal, primitive compactness, frozen T018 smoothing \taglocal,
and Asano--Ike Theorem~1.1 \tagsource. Internally verified only.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}
Finite $2$-variation only; not all Jordan curves. Critical $p=2$ modulus covered separately (frozen T020).

\section{Simple Jordan interpolation \tagsource}
Boedihardjo--Geng Theorem~2.2 yields simple chord polygons $P_n=c^{D_n}$ with mesh $\to0$.

\section{T021: Simple-arc area bound \taglocal}
\begin{theorem}[T021]
$|\mathcal A_\alpha(s,t)|\le\frac{\pi}{4}|\alpha|_{2\text{-var};[s,t]}^2$ for simple polygonal arcs.
\end{theorem}
Chord--lobe decomposition; isodiametric bound; degenerate limits by subdivision.

\section{Local $2$-variation control \taglocal}
$\omega_c(s,t)=|c|_{2\text{-var};[s,t]}^2$ is superadditive and continuous. PL variation controlled at partition nodes.

\section{Primitive equicontinuity and Arzel\`a--Ascoli \taglocal}
$f_n(t)-f_n(s)=\frac{\xi_n(s)+\xi_n(t)}{2}(x_n(t)-x_n(s))-\mathcal A_{P_n}(s,t)$. Extract $f_{n_k}\to f$ uniformly.

\section{T018 smoothing \taglocal}
Velocity-matched cubic corners; adaptive tolerance for diverging polygon length (frozen release).

\section{Lift and Asano--Ike \tagsource}
Period-count estimate; Theorem~1.1 and Remark~5.6.

\section{Limitations}
Depends on Asano--Ike preprint. Infinite $2$-variation not covered (T023 open).
