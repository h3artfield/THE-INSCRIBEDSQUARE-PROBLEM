## Statement

If a Jordan curve admits an **area-tight** sequence of simple polygonal approximants (uniform convergence + vanishing relative area on shrinking parameter pairs), then it satisfies Asano–Ike Theorem 1.1 and inscribes every prescribed rectangle.

---

## Proof chain

1. Polygon primitives \(f_n(t)=\int_0^t\xi_n\,dx_n\) — trapezoid identity;
2. Uniform curve bound + area-tightness \(\Rightarrow\) primitive equicontinuity;
3. Arzelà–Ascoli subsequence \(f_{n_k}\to f\);
4. T018 adaptive smoothing preserves primitive limit;
5. Asano–Ike.
