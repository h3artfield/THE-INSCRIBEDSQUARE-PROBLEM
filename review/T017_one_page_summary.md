## Theorem (T017)

Jordan curve \(c:S^1\to\mathbb R^2\) with finite \(p\)-variation for some \(1\le p<2\) inscribes every \(\theta\)-rectangle (\(\theta\in(0,\pi)\)), via Asano–Ike Theorem 1.1.

---

## Why not identical to rectifiable case

Finite \(p\)-var for \(p<2\) includes **nonrectifiable** curves (e.g. Koch-type). Rectifiability = finite 1-variation.
